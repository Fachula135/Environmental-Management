The subject

What is IGCSE Environmental Management?

Not memorising facts. You use data, case studies and decisions to answer: how can people use the planet without wrecking it? Nine topics, and today we do the one in yellow.

The ocean

Bigger than we thought. Weaker than we thought.

71%of the Earth surface is ocean
~10%of the ocean is shallow shelf, where most commercial fish live
75%+of marine fisheries are threatened (coursebook)
Halfof a huge net catch can be thrown away as bycatch

Game

Run the fishery

You run a fishing town for six seasons. Nothing is simple: every decision pushes three things at once (ocean health, income and community trust), and some bets go wrong. Keep all three alive to win. If any one hits zero, you lose.
